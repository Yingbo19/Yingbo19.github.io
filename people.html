Yingbo Yang

Macroecology · Invasion Ecology · Biodiversity Conservation

I work at the College of Ecology, Lanzhou University. Before returning to Lanzhou, I completed a three-year postdoctoral appointment at the Department of Biology, Aarhus University, within the Center for Ecological Dynamics in a Novel Biosphere (ECONOVO).

I am interested in how biological invasions and global environmental change reshape biodiversity. My research combines species distribution data and spatial modelling to study where alien plants establish, how their climatic niches change, and what these changes mean for conservation.

Beyond invasions, I investigate the ecological and evolutionary processes underlying large-scale plant diversity, including niche conservatism, mountain biodiversity and divergence between closely related species.
