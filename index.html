Welcome

I study how climate, human activities and evolutionary history shape the distribution of plants and the diversity of life.

I am based at the College of Ecology, Lanzhou University, China. I recently returned to Lanzhou after completing three years of postdoctoral research at Aarhus University in Denmark.

My work brings together biodiversity records, spatial modelling and ecological theory to understand plant invasions, climatic niche dynamics and biodiversity conservation, from counties across China to mountain systems around the world.
